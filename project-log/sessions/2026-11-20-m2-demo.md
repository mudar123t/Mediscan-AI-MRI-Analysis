- README "Local demo" section, with the figure `images/demo_outputs.png` (demo outputs for one uncertain and one confident example).

## Findings
- The demo shows the mechanism clearly: the uncertain mask (u 0.667) goes to the full image, the confident one (u 0.020) to the crop.
- 3 of the 5 examples are referred at α = 0.1, all with empty conformal sets (confidence 0.54–0.68). Two of them are meningiomas that were misclassified as pituitary (rows 185 and 8), so the referral catches both errors. The third (row 9) is correct but low-confidence (0.62).
